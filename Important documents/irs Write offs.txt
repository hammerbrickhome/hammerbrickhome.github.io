# ✅ **Allowed Write-Off Categories for Your LLC (Even With Zero Profit)**

These are standard IRS Schedule C categories. You only deduct **business-related** expenses — not personal.

If your business is new or still building up:

* Forming the LLC
* Legal fees
* Website setup
* Marketing materials
* Consulting fees
* Accountant fees
* Research, training, courses
* Equipment bought during startup

**IRS allows immediate deduction up to $5,000** for startup.

---

# 🔹 **2. Home Office Deduction**

If you use any area **exclusively** for business:

* Home office flat rate: *$5 per sq ft (max 300 sq ft)*

✔️ Must show you are **actually running a real business**

# ✅ **IRS Deduction Percentages for Each Category**

### **✔ Materials – 100%**

✔ Tools – 100%
✔ Job Supplies – 100%
✔ Subcontractor Payments – 100%
✔ Rental Equipment – 100%
✔ Marketing / Advertising – 100%
✔ Advertisements (Google Ads, Instagram Ads) – 100%
✔ Website / Software – 100%
✔ Insurance – 100%
✔ Licenses / Fees – 100%
✔ Miscellaneous business expenses – 100%
✔ Equipment (Section 179 or bonus depreciation) – Up to **100%**
✔ Bank fees – 100%
✔ Postage / shipping – 100%
✔ Training / certifications – 100%
✔ Business phone line – **100% of the business portion**

---

# 🛞 **Vehicle-Related Deductions**

You choose ONE of two methods:

## **1️⃣ Standard Mileage Deduction**

IRS gives you:

### **➜ 67¢ per mile (2024 rate)**

This includes fuel, repairs, insurance, maintenance, everything.

OR

## **2️⃣ Actual Expense Method**

You deduct the **business-use %** of:

* Fuel
* Insurance
* Repairs
* Depreciation
* Car washes
* Tires
* Registration fees

Example:
If 70% of miles are business → deduct 70% of car costs.

---

# 🧾 **Gas / Fuel**

If using **actual expense method**:

### ➜ **Deduct the business percentage (%).**

Example:
If 60% of total miles were business → 60% of gas is deductible.

---

# 🚧 **Tolls**

### ✔ **100% deductible**

(Only for business trips)

---

# 🍔 **Meals**

IRS rule:

### **➜ 50% deductible**

Business meals only.
Note who you met + why (quick note on receipt).

---

# 🏡 **Home Office Deduction**

Two methods:

### **1️⃣ Simplified Method**

* $5 per sq ft
* Max 300 sq ft
* Max $1,500/year

### **2️⃣ Actual Expense Method**

You deduct the % of your home used only for business:

* Rent or mortgage interest
* Utilities
* Electricity
* Internet (business % only)
* Repairs to the office area

Example:
Home office is 10% of your apartment → deduct 10% of these bills.

---

# ☁️ **Internet**

* Deduct the **business-use %**
  Example:
  If you use WiFi 70% for business → deduct 70%.

---

# 📱 **Phone Bill**

IRS rule:

* Your **first phone line is personal**, cannot deduct 100%
* But you CAN deduct **the business-use %**

Example:
If 60% of calls are business → deduct 60%.

If you have a **second line** for business → 100% deductible.

---

# 🛠 **Equipment**

Two systems:

### **1️⃣ Section 179 – Up to 100% same year**

Most contractors use this.

### **2️⃣ Bonus Depreciation – Up to 100%**

(Some items depreciate over 5–7 years)

---

# 📚 **Training & Certifications**

### ✔ **100% deductible**

(IRS considers it “education to maintain or improve business skills.”)

---

# ❗ VERY IMPORTANT IRS RULE (For You With $145K Job)

You CAN claim a **business loss** even if your LLC made **$0** or a small amount.

Expenses can reduce your taxable income from your W-2 job **as long as it’s a real business** and you keep records.
