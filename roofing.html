Flat Roof Systems

We assess existing membrane condition, drainage, parapets, seams and roof penetrations before discussing repair or replacement. Projects may involve rubber/EPDM, modified-bitumen and other roofing systems appropriate to the building.

Shingle Roofing

For sloped roofs, we look at missing or damaged shingles, flashing, underlying decking and ventilation. Material and installation options are confirmed during the on-site assessment.

Leaks, Flashing & Waterproofing

Water often enters at penetrations, drains, chimneys, edges or wall transitions. We work to identify the entry point rather than automatically recommending a complete replacement.
