OUR WORKMANSHIP STANDARD

Our Standards & Written Warranty

A clear project is built on a clear scope, careful protection, documented work and written expectations.

What you can expect

We define the agreed scope in writing before work begins. Materials, access, exclusions, payment terms and project-specific warranty language belong in the written estimate or contract.

Workmanship

Our goal is clean, durable work completed according to the written scope. We review completed work with the customer and address agreed punch-list items before final closeout.

Manufacturer warranties

Some products may carry separate manufacturer warranties. Those warranties are governed by the manufacturer and the specific product installed.

Hidden conditions

Existing water damage, concealed deterioration, structural movement, pest damage, code issues and other concealed conditions can change the work required. If something unexpected is discovered, we document it and discuss options before additional work is authorized.

Your written agreement controls

This page explains our general approach. The signed project agreement and project-specific warranty language control the actual job.
