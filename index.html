A Clear Process From First Call to Final Walkthrough

  1. 1. Quick Call, Text, or Photos

    You reach out however is easiest — call, text, or send a few photos of the area. We ask simple questions about your home, timeline, and budget so we know if you need a small repair, a safety fix, or a full remodel.

  2. 2. Clear, Written Estimate

    If needed, we schedule a walk-through to measure, check conditions, and spot any hidden issues before they become surprises. You receive a written, itemized estimate with Foundation, Premium, and Signature options so you can see what each tier includes and exactly what it costs.

  3. 3. Protection & Prep

    Before tools come out, floors, railings, and walkways are covered with paper, plastic, and drop cloths. We set up dust control, safe walking paths, and protect nearby finishes so the rest of your home stays clean and livable while we work.

  4. 4. Build, Inspect, Communicate

    Our licensed crew performs the work according to manufacturer specs and NYC-ready code awareness. You get updates during the project — what we did today, what’s happening next — and we do internal checks to make sure everything is straight, level, and properly tied into the existing structure.

  5. 5. Final Walk-Through & After Photos

    We walk the project with you, note any touch-ups, and handle them before we call the job finished. The area is swept, wiped, and ready for photos. You leave with before/after documentation and simple care tips so your new work stays looking sharp for years.
