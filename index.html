QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Straight answers before you call.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Call us directly or text photos of the project. If the scope needs an in-person look, we can schedule a site visit.

Are you licensed and insured?

Hammer Brick & Home LLC is licensed, bonded and insured. NYC HIC #2131291 and NJ HIC #13VH14026000.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Staten Island and serve the five boroughs of New York City plus select New Jersey areas. Call or text to confirm your location.

Can I send photos before scheduling a visit?

Yes. Texting clear photos is one of the fastest ways to start. Include your location and a short description of what you want repaired or remodeled.

What about work in pre-1978 homes?

Hammer Brick & Home LLC is an EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm. Applicable projects are evaluated for lead-safe work requirements.

Who handles permits?

Permit requirements depend on the project. When permits are applicable, the written proposal or contract should identify the permitting responsibility and scope.
