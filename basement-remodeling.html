Preparation & Demolition

Basement projects may involve removing damaged finishes, selective demolition, subfloor evaluation and preparing the space for new framing. Existing conditions are assessed before work begins.

Framing, Drywall & Insulation

We plan wall framing, moisture-resistant finishes where appropriate, fire-rated materials where required and clean interior trim. Materials depend on site conditions and applicable requirements.

Flooring, Lighting & Finishes

Options may include tile, appropriate resilient flooring, wall finishing, Benjamin Moore paint and coordinated electrical or plumbing work through properly authorized trades.

Moisture Comes First

If there are signs of water infiltration or recurring dampness, address the source before concealing surfaces. The best finish materials will not solve an untreated leak.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you finish a basement in Staten Island?

Yes. We visit Staten Island basement projects and select sites throughout NYC and nearby New Jersey to discuss scope and conditions.

Should waterproofing happen before framing?

Any active water intrusion should be evaluated and corrected before new walls and flooring conceal the affected surfaces.

Is a permit needed to remodel a basement?

Permit and design requirements vary by intended use and work involved. We assess the proposed changes and coordinate appropriate professionals when filings are required.
