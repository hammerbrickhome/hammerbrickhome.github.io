PRE-1978 HOME PROTECTION

Lead-Safe Renovation for Pre-1978 Homes

Older homes can require additional containment, cleanup and lead-safe work practices when painted surfaces are disturbed.

Why pre-1978 homes are different

Lead-based paint may be present in older homes. Renovation work that disturbs painted surfaces can create dust, so the work plan should account for the age and condition of the home.

Lead-safe work practices

When applicable, work may include controlled work areas, plastic containment, dust control, HEPA vacuuming, careful cleanup and verification procedures.

Scope and cost

Lead-safe requirements can affect labor, setup, cleanup and scheduling. Any project-specific requirements should be included in the written scope.

Ask before demolition begins

If you are unsure about the age of the property or previous testing, tell us during the estimate so the project can be planned correctly.
